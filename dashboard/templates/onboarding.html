Step 1: Deploy the Audit Role in Your AWS Account

Click the button below to deploy a read-only IAM Role in your AWS account. This role gives our tool permission to view your resources — it cannot make any changes.

What gets created:
  • An IAM Role named AuditManagerRole
  • Read-only permissions (SecurityAudit + ReadOnlyAccess)
  • Trust policy allowing only account {{ tool_account_id }} to assume it
  • No write access — we cannot modify your resources
Launch in AWS Console

Step 2: Copy the Role ARN

After the CloudFormation stack finishes (takes ~1 minute), find the Role ARN:

  1. Go to AWS CloudFormation Console
  2. Click the AuditManagerRole stack
  3. Click the Outputs tab
  4. Copy the RoleARN value

It looks like: arn:aws:iam::YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID:role/AuditManagerRole
